About this book

On the desert planet Arrakis, the only source of the most precious substance in the known universe, House Atreides takes charge of a world it barely understands. When the family is betrayed, young Paul Atreides escapes into the deep desert and finds refuge among the Fremen, whose endurance and long-held hopes begin to reshape his future.

Herbert builds an intricate world of shifting alliances, scarce water and carefully planted prophecy, asking what happens when ecology, empire and belief collide. Part adventure and part political thriller, it remains one of the most influential novels in science fiction.
